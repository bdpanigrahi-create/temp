Incident ID: INC-20260519-10
Title: Distributed Deadlock in Inventory Management
Date: 2026-05-19
Severity: HIGH

Symptom:
Inventory updates hanging, resulting in timeouts and failed orders during peak hours.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA):
Two services (Order Service and Inventory Service) were acquiring locks on 'Product' and 'Warehouse' resources in reverse order. Service A locked Product then Warehouse; Service B locked Warehouse then Product, causing a classic distributed deadlock.

Remediation Steps:
Short-term:
Restarted the deadlocked service instances to release the held locks.

Long-term:
Enforced a strict resource locking order across all services and implemented lock timeouts to prevent indefinite hangs.
