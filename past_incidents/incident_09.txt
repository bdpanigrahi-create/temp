Incident ID: INC-20260517-9
Title: Message Queue Consumer Livelock
Date: 2026-05-17
Severity: HIGH

Symptom:
Messages in the order processing queue were not being processed, but consumer instances appeared healthy and running.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA):
A specific malformed message caused an unhandled exception in the consumer. Since the consumer acknowledged messages *after* processing, it crashed before acknowledgment. The message broker redelivered it immediately, causing an infinite loop of crashes and redeliveries (livelock).

Remediation Steps:
Short-term:
Identified and manually moved the problematic message to a dead-letter queue.

Long-term:
Implemented a Dead-Letter Queue (DLQ) for messages that fail processing more than 3 times. Added global exception handling to consumers.
