Incident ID: INC-20260511-6
Title: Outage due to Expired SSL Certificate on Load Balancer
Date: 2026-05-11
Severity: CRITICAL

Symptom:
Users unable to access the application, receiving 'Connection not secure' warnings in their browsers.

Root Cause Analysis (RCA):
The automated renewal script for the Let's Encrypt certificate failed because the HTTP-01 challenge path was blocked by a new routing rule deployed the previous week, which redirected all traffic to a maintenance page during a window.

Remediation Steps:
Short-term:
Manually renewed the certificate using a DNS challenge and updated the load balancer configuration.

Long-term:
Updated the routing rules to explicitly exclude the ACME challenge path. Added external monitoring to check certificate expiry dates.
